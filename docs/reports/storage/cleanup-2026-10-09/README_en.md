## Criteria and Important Exceptions

Deletion bounded to explicitly replaced intermediate IPR archives by validated corrections in October 2 reports. No active file is deleted merely because it is old or large.

- Whites/Highlights: delete v1 and positive v2 branch replaced by v3; **preserve the eight negative v2 of each family**, still used for v3 verdict and absent from active folders.
- Temperature: delete old cold v1/v2; **preserve the eight warm v1**, whose branch has not changed and are absent from the active folder.
- Contrast/Tint: delete variants prior to validated corrections; preserve active exports.
- Blacks/Shadows: delete states before separation and post-separation v1; preserve final validation archives and all their references.
- Mixer: preserve extremes and intermediates, which do not constitute the same campaign. Identical names in these series do not prove content duplicates.
- Noise, ZIP of neutrals, and other ambiguous files: preserve. No exhaustive pixel-identical duplicate scan has been performed.

Decision sources: analyses [Blacks/Shadows](../../../validation/analyses/analyse-noirs-ombres-2026-10-02.md), [Whites/HL](../../../validation/analyses/analyse-blancs-hautes-lumieres-2026-10-02.md), [Contrast](../../../validation/analyses/analyse-contraste-2026-10-02.md), [Temperature](../../../validation/analyses/analyse-temperature-2026-10-02.md), and [Tint](../../../validation/analyses/analyse-nuance-2026-10-02.md).

**Permanent deletion, without recycling bin.** Fingerprints and preserved measurements are not image backups. Old TIFFs can only be recovered from a possible external backup or regenerated by reproducing their old engine/configuration. Historical conclusions remain consultable, but some intermediate measurements can no longer be relaunched identically from the local corpus.

For the next campaign: one feature at a time, identified new neutrals, then preservation of measurements and only useful exports. The script refuses a second execution if this audit exists: it is not an automatic purge tool for future images.
