# Reports and Archives

Reports are historical evidence, not the functional reference of the product.

- `sessions/` receives all new session reports, named `session-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- `archive/` receives journals that have become inactive or consolidated.
- Measurement campaigns and their protocols belong to `../validation/`.
- Inventories and corpus cleanup belong to `storage/`; see the [October 9 balance](storage/cleanup-2026-10-09/README.md).
- The [Lightroom cleanup of October 9](storage/lightroom-cleanup-2026-10-09/README.md) removes Adobe Standard references and preserves Camera Neutral.
- The [build cleanup of October 10](storage/build-cleanup-2026-10-10/README.md) removes five unnecessary folders and preserves active builds and useful diagnostics.
- Historical families were migrated on October 3, 2026; their correspondence is preserved in [migration-registry.md](migration-registry.md).

Before closing a session, update a specification, the validation matrix, and an ADR when the session has produced a durable decision.

The [October 8 consolidation](consolidation-2026-10-08.csv) preserves the fingerprints of the seven grouped mixer/noise reports, their destinations, and campaign anchors. Old paths remain as references. Dated session logs and raw measurements are not merged with current conclusions.
